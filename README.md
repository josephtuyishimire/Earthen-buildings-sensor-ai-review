# Earthen-buildings-sensor-ai-review
# Sensor AI Integration in Structural and Hygrothermal Monitoring of Earthen Buildings

## About This Project

This repository provides the research dataset and supporting analytical materials for the systematic review entitled **“A Systematic Review of Sensor AI Integration in Structural and Hygrothermal Monitoring of Earthen Buildings.”**

The review investigates how sensing technologies and artificial intelligence are applied to structural and hygrothermal monitoring of earthen buildings, with particular attention to the integration of monitoring systems, the maturity of AI applications, validation evidence, and opportunities for further research.

## Why This Project Was Developed

Earthen construction offers opportunities for sustainable building development because of its use of natural materials and potential thermal performance benefits. However, earthen buildings are sensitive to environmental conditions, including moisture fluctuations, temperature variations, and structural deterioration.

Advances in sensing technologies, Internet of Things (IoT) systems, and artificial intelligence offer new opportunities to monitor building conditions, analyse sensor measurements, detect changes, and support predictive assessment.

Despite these technological developments, the available research remains distributed across different monitoring objectives, sensor configurations, AI techniques, and validation approaches.

**This project was developed to systematically organise and evaluate that evidence**, identify the current state of Sensor AI integration, assess technological maturity, and establish research gaps relevant to reliable monitoring of earthen buildings.

## Research Objectives

The systematic review focuses on five interconnected areas:

1. **Monitoring technologies:** Identify sensors and measurement approaches used for structural and hygrothermal monitoring.
2. **Artificial intelligence integration:** Examine how AI techniques are combined with sensor measurements for analysis, prediction, and monitoring.
3. **Technological maturity:** Assess the maturity of monitoring and intelligent analysis systems.
4. **Structural and hygrothermal integration:** Investigate the extent to which both monitoring domains are addressed together.
5. **Multidepth sensing and energy applications:** Examine the use of measurements collected at different wall depths and their connection to building energy performance prediction.

## Dataset Overview

The review screening process covered **608 records**, from which **295 unique records** were identified. Following screening and eligibility assessment, **98 studies** were included in the systematic review.

The accompanying Excel Master Workbook contains structured research information used to support the review, including:

- Study identification and extracted information
- Sensor technologies and monitoring applications
- Artificial intelligence models and integration approaches
- Structural and hygrothermal monitoring classifications
- Monitoring maturity assessments
- Validation evidence classifications
- Multidepth sensing and energy related applications
- Quality assessment and supporting analysis tables

The dataset provides an organised evidence base for understanding technological developments and identifying limitations in the existing literature.

## Purpose of Sharing the Dataset

The Excel dataset is shared to support:

- **Research transparency:** Make the evidence underlying the systematic review accessible.
- **Reproducibility:** Enable other researchers to inspect the extracted data and analytical classifications.
- **Independent verification:** Facilitate examination of monitoring technologies, AI integration, and validation evidence.
- **Future research:** Provide a structured foundation for comparative studies, research updates, and further investigation.
- **Scientific collaboration:** Encourage researchers working in sustainable construction, structural monitoring, building physics, IoT, and artificial intelligence to build on the evidence.
